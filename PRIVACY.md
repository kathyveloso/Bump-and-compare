# Privacy

**Your check-ins stay on your device.** Bump & Compare stores your entries in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them.

- **No accounts, no tracking.** The app has no sign-up, analytics or advertising trackers.
- **Fonts.** The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives a standard web request (including your IP address) when the page opens.
- **Links.** Source links open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.
- **Deleting your data.** Clear this site's data in your browser settings. This permanently deletes your log.
- **Losing your data.** If you clear your browser, use private browsing or switch device, your log won't come with you.

This app is a prototype for testing and is not medical advice. If you're worried about your health or your baby, contact your midwife, maternity unit or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.
