When to get help

      
Call your midwife or maternity triage, or NHS 111 out of hours, if you can't keep fluids down for 24 hours, are weeing less or it's dark, feel dizzy or faint, are losing weight, or feel very low or anxious most days. Call your maternity unit straight away for any bleeding or severe tummy pain, or later in pregnancy if the baby is moving less. In an emergency call 999.
